前言

本隱私權政策說明 PinVector（以下稱「本 App」）會處理哪些資料、資料的用途、會提供給哪些第三方服務，以及您擁有的權利。本 App 由 ChaoYiTing（以下稱「我們」）開發與營運。

本 App 不需要註冊帳號，我們沒有自己的伺服器。您的定位紀錄、最愛與路線只儲存在您的裝置上，我們無法讀取。

1. 我們處理的資料

1.1 儲存在您裝置上的資料

資料用途儲存位置

使用次數、廣告獎勵次數、VIP 狀態、App 設定次數限制、免廣告判斷、保存偏好僅儲存在裝置上

1.2 由第三方服務處理的資料

為提供地圖、路線、廣告、付款與 App 品質改善，本 App 使用下列第三方服務。這些服務會依其各自的隱私權政策處理資料：

服務處理的資料用途

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what data PinVector ("the App") handles, why, which third-party services receive it, and your rights. The App is developed and operated by ChaoYiTing ("we", "us").

The App does not require an account, and we do not operate our own servers. Your location history, favorites and routes are stored only on your device, and we cannot access them.

2. Permissions

  • Location: showing your current position on the map.
  • Mock location (Developer options): providing the location you choose to other apps on the device.
  • Foreground service and notifications: keeping the simulation running and showing its status and controls in the notification.
  • Internet: loading maps, looking up routes and addresses, ads, payments and Firebase services.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob to serve and measure ads.

3. Ads and your choices

  • The free version shows ads, which may be personalized based on your advertising ID and usage. VIP users do not see ads.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or certain US states, the App shows a consent message as required by law. You can change your choice at any time in the App's privacy settings.
  • You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (for example Settings > Privacy > Ads; names vary by device).

4. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device is kept until you delete it: delete or clear records on the App's History page, clear the App's data in system settings, or uninstall the App.
  • Third-party services retain data under their own policies. We set Firebase Analytics data retention to 14 months; Crashlytics crash logs are retained as specified by Firebase (currently 90 days).
  • You may contact us to request deletion of Firebase data associated with your installation ID.

5. Your rights

  • Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA or Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data.
  • Because the App has no accounts and no servers of our own, most data stays on your device, and you can manage it directly in the App or in system settings.
  • For any other request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

Communication between the App and third-party services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data on your device is protected by Android's app data isolation.

7. Children

The App is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us and we will help delete it.

8. Responsibility for mock location use

The App only changes the location your device provides to other apps. The terms of some apps, games or services may prohibit mock locations. You are responsible for checking and following those terms, and for any consequences of using the App.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features or laws change. We will update the effective date on this page, and notify you in the App or on the store listing of significant changes.
